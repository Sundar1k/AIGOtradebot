How the AlgoBot Places a Trade (LONG example)

Real geometry from config.py — STOP_ATR=0.5 · RR=2.0 · ACTIVATE_R=2.0 · GIVEBACK_R=0.75
Entry — signal must clear confidence floor ≥0.35, pass the 7B veto, and pass the chop gate
Stop Loss (SL) = 0.5 × ATR(20) below entry — that's your fixed 1R risk
Take Profit (TP) = 2 × the stop distance (2R) — risk $1 to make $2
Exit trail — hold initial stop until +2R seen, then ratchet to breakeven and trail at peak − 0.75R
In plain words: the bot risks one fixed unit (0.5×ATR). If wrong, it stops out for −1R. If right, it lets the winner run to 2R, then flips the stop to breakeven so the trade can't turn into a loss, and trails it upward to capture as much as possible. That's why its wins are often +0.3R to +2R: the ratchet protects profit but can exit early on a pullback.
