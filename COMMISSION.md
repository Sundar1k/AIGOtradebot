## What counts

You owe 5% of "Net Trading Profits": your gross realized trading gains,
minus the direct costs of making those trades (broker commissions/fees,
exchange fees, funding, slippage, data/API fees). It is calculated
before your personal income tax. Losses in one quarter are not carried
forward to offset gains in another.

the 5% within 30 days after that quarter ends.
